01 / About

About Me

I am an Interior Design student at the University of New Haven with a passion for creating spaces that are functional, welcoming, and meaningful.

My interests include accessibility, space planning, materials and color, visual communication, and understanding how people interact with the built environment.

Through my college coursework, I am continuing to strengthen both my creative and technical design abilities while developing my own identity as a designer.

Disaster Relief Housing

An ADA-conscious disaster-relief housing concept designed for a wildfire-prone Los Angeles setting.

The project explores accessibility, functionality, safety, site conditions, and fire-resistant materials while creating a comfortable residential environment.

My contributions include site planning, exterior and interior renderings, technical drawings, and material research.

Interior Design Studio II · Spring 2026

Pediatric Office Design

A pediatric office concept inspired by the Dennys River and the surrounding Maine landscape.

Natural materials and a palette of greens, browns, and blues were used to create a connection between the interior and its location.

The project also explores child-friendly wayfinding through floating leaf imagery and nature-inspired graphics.

  • Photoshop
  • Wayfinding
  • Color Theory
  • Hand Rendering
